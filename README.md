# 🌊 Sessions — Surfer's Log

A beautiful, private surf tracking web app built for surfers who want to log sessions, track progress, and visualize their journey toward mastery.

---

## 📱 Live App

👉 **[Open Sessions](https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/sessions-surfers-log/)**

---

## ✨ Features

- **Log Sessions** — spot, rating, duration, waves caught, board used, swell conditions, buddies & photos
- **Session History** — year-at-a-glance line chart + 365-day surf heatmap
- **10,000-Hour Journey** — track your path toward mastery
- **Stats** — waves caught, countries surfed, breaks surfed, boards broken
- **Shortboard Goal** — set an hour target, track progress automatically
- **Quiver** — manage your board quiver
- **Breaks & Regs** — notes on your favourite spots
- **Surf Buddies** — see who you've surfed with most
- **Interactive Map** — every break you've surfed on a map
- **100% Private** — all data stored locally on your device, never uploaded anywhere

---

## 🔒 Privacy

Sessions stores all your data in your browser's local storage. No accounts, no servers, no cloud sync. Your sessions are yours alone.

> Note: Data is device-specific. Creating an account on a new device starts fresh.
